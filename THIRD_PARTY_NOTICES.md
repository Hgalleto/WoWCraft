# Third-party data used by WoWCraft

## ForeverDB.net
Item sources, recipe skill levels, reagents and vendor lists in `wowcraft/web/items.json` and `wowcraft/web/crafting.json` come from the ForeverDB addon's data files.

**Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)**

ForeverDB's data is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 with an attribution term (GPL-3.0 section 7(b)): you may reuse it if you keep the notices and credit "Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)". Sources ForeverDB labels as Classic data come from the CMaNGOS classic-db project (GPL-3.0). WoWCraft shows those as "not yet seen in Forever".

## talentsforever.com
Talent trees, ranks, tooltips, racials (data export, CC BY 4.0) and the recommended spec builds (popular builds). Data from talentsforever.com (https://talentsforever.com).

## Wowhead
Item stats, icons and tooltip text from Wowhead's WoW Forever tooltips; the 3D model viewer and model files.

## Blizzard Entertainment
WoW Forever client tables (via wago.tools), icons, tree art and models are Blizzard Entertainment's. WoWCraft is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment, ForeverDB, talentsforever.com or Wowhead.
